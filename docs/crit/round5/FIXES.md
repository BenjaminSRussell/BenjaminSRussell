## F1. The sheet (scripts/sheets/hero.py, tests/test_hero.py, chart.toml [hero])

1. **Weekly bins, not months** (data). A week with commit-days is land for that week only; half-thickness is
   linear in commit-days that week (0–7), one scale for the sheet, the busiest week filling about 0.8 of the
   row pitch on both editions; a single-day week is a small islet, not a bar. No thickness floor that makes one
   day look like a month. Rows keep the sweep exclusion.
2. **Land and water** (navigator, student, tone). A shallows band of water tint (THEMES shallow tint, night variant,
   contrast-checked) about 6 px wide along every coastline, so buff land, blue shallows and pale water read as a
   chart at a glance. No new words.
3. **The rows add up to 21** (all seven). The profile repository (BenjaminSRussell) is not a named row; it joins
   the last row. The last row counts every repository not named, including those with only bulk-edit days, and
   is lettered `N MORE` so that named rows + N = repo_count. Draw its land from those with days.
4. **A figure on every row** (infodesign, data). The row's commit-days (sweep days out) right-aligned on the name
   line in figures, `DAYS` after the first row's figure only. Upright, measured, keyed.
5. **Language tags on every named row** from the largest language by lines in the clone (data key `main_language`,
   fallback: the largest key of `lines`), never from the REST `language` field.
6. **The light is `Fl 30s`** (skeptic, verified): the crawler's own Prometheus job (`scrapy_app`) scrapes every 30 s;
   15 s is only the global default. Read the period from `claims.scrape_interval` (the data builder re-points it);
   never hard-code it. Place the light on Scrapy's latest bank (it describes the code as it is now), 7 px desk,
   9 px phone. Beat once per period. Adjust checks/frames.py's hero window in the same commit if needed.
7. **Fine print** (hiring, tone): drop `AUTHOR'S COMMITS` and `SWEEP DAYS EXCLUDED` from the sheet; the page's
   foot line defines them. Keep `21 REPOSITORIES · DATUM: MAIN` and `<date> · EASTERN TIME`.
8. **Frame clearances** (navigator, skeptic, student): the axis ends 14 px inside the border; drop the partial final
   month letter and put a tick at the survey date lettered with the date (`9 OCT`); desk rows start ≥ 24 px below the
   top border; the desk URL line sits fully inside the sheet.
9. **Phone hierarchy** (navigator): role line 30 px in ink, a 12 px gap, the fine print at the 26 px floor in
   secondary ink.
10. **Alt text** (tone): no "coastline"; say what it shows in plain words.
