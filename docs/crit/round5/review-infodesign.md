## 3. What must change

1. **Thickness carries nothing, again.** 13 of the 21 charted months hold 1–3 commit-days, and the 3 px floor draws them all as one 6 px sliver inside 42 px rows that are about 85 % paper (sheet-desk-day-870). Fix: set the name on the row's own baseline, in the empty water left of or above its first bank. Pitch 30 px desk / 34 px phone. Half-thickness 1 px per commit-day, floor 1.5 px, so Scrapy's Oct 2025 (15 days) is 15 px of real land. Draw a plain 0.75 px coast under 4 px half-thickness, no ragged edge. Then the ink sits where the work was: a coastline of a year, not seven threads. That is the screenshot.
2. **No figure anywhere.** A stranger cannot tell what a bank measures. Fix: right-align the figure on each name line at the axis end, `SCRAPY PYTHON ··· 25 DAYS`, then `19`, `11`… with "DAYS" on the first row only. The unit explains itself.
3. **The rows don't add up.** The title says 21 repositories, the sheet shows 7 + `9 MORE`, and five repositories with no day outside the sweeps vanish. Fix: letter the merged row `14 MORE`. True, and it sums.
4. **The light reads as a glitch.** At 390 the magenta star is a 4 px pink blot on Scrapy's Oct 2025 bank (page-phone-1). A scrape interval is a property of the code now, so put the light on the row's latest bank (Sep–Oct 2026), 9 px phone / 7 px desk, `Fl 15s` beside it.
5. **Page.** (a) `Generated from my repositories by scripts/build_assets.py` breaks the standing rule against "generated by" footers. Cut it; `DESIGN.md` joins the license line. (b) The caption under the image repeats the sheet's role line and wraps `Python and / Rust` on the phone. Cut it. (c) The D4 and provenance lines are `<sub>`, set at double leading by GitHub (page-phone-2). Use `<p><small>`. (d) The axis's final `O` touches the border in every render. Inset it 6 px.
