## 2. The five-second test

**870 px, day.** A serif name the size of a masthead, an italic sentence about surveying a wrong web, and a chart: blue tints, a compass clock, islands with depths, a hatched right margin saying UNSURVEYED. In five seconds the page says: a person, a thesis, a chart he made. The field arrives at second four from the small-caps title block and at second six from the table ("Ben Russell builds crawl and data infrastructure"). One hazard: the largest legible feature label on the chart is GAME ENGINE I., with SCRAPY HARBOR second; a five-second reader could file him as a games person before the table corrects it. Nothing says "Scrapy maintainer".

**360 px.** hero-phone is a redrawn edition, not a shrink: name, thesis on two lines, GAME ENGINE I., SCRAPY HARBOR, Profile Shoal, Data Science Bank, UNSURVEYED and SMALL-SCALE rotated at the edge, and a title block that reads THE OPEN WEB · CRAWL AND DATA INFRASTRUCTURE · PYTHON AND RUST. Name and thesis land in under three seconds; field in five. No light is visible on the phone hero (a nun and a can, no star), which is the one STD 5 phone element missing.

**Night.** The name goes pale on navy, the thesis stays italic, the chart becomes dark water with gray land; the only warm things are the boat's sail and two pink dots at Scrapy Harbor. It reads as a night chart, not an inverted day one, and the person reads the same: name, thesis, survey. The GAME ENGINE island reads as a dark crater, which is louder at night than by day.

## 3. Top 5 defects, ranked

**1. The page has no ending. The footer is inside a collapsed `<details>` that is itself cut mid-sentence.**
Where: readme-light.png / readme-dark.png, bottom; last visible element at y≈4930 is "▶ Colophon · how the sheets are drawn: type, editions, motion, variation, license". Open it and the Editions bullet ends at "Your system picks the edition through `" and is followed by the footer `<picture>`; the Motion, Variation, Figures and License bullets are gone (compare README.md lines 190–209 with docs/crit/tech/T8-README.final.md lines 163–169). The render's `<details>` is never closed.
Why it matters: STD 7 (the footer is where the water finally goes over the edge; there is no climax-to-edge arc without it), STD 13 (the serpent and the colophon's variation resolution both live here), J3/J8/J12. In my panel document the footer was act three of a three-act structure that begins with the avatar; as built, act three exists as a file and not as a page. The last thing a reader sees is a disclosure triangle, which is the most template-shaped ending possible.
Fix: in scripts/render_readme.py the literal `<picture>` inside the Editions bullet is being taken as the footer picture slot. Reword the bullet to "picks the edition through the `picture` element", or escape it, and emit the footer picture only after the closing `</details>`. Add a gate line to check.py: the rendered README must contain balanced `<details>` and its last `<picture>` must be the footer. Note: README.md on disk was repaired while this review ran (colophon complete at lines 197–203, `</details>` at 206, footer picture at 210–219); the judged render in qa/readme/ still shows the truncated page, so the fix is unconfirmed until readme-light.png and readme-dark.png are regenerated and the gate line above passes.
Cost: S.

**2. The collectible loop is still one-directional, and the Notices list dilutes its own reward.**
Where: hero-day bottom margin, x≈650–860,y≈495: "SMALL CORRECTIONS 2026 — 169, 170, 171, 172, 173". Approaches x≈160–310,y≈465: "Local knowledge advised · see Notices 1–5". README Notices section (readme-light-3, y≈480–935): heading says *five principles*, the list has nine entries, 6–9 being four PyPI releases dated the same day, and no entry sends the reader back up.
Why it matters: STD 13 (marginal notes tied to the Notices), J12. The margin tally uses numbers that match nothing on the page (the notices are 1–9), so a reader who goes looking finds no correspondence; the chart points down, the list never points up; and "These five I had to be told" is followed by nine lines, four of which are a changelog. A collectible that never makes you scroll back is a caption; a list of five that is nine is a list of nine.
Fix: (a) make the tally read the notice numbers the chart actually carries, "SMALL CORRECTIONS 2026 — 1, 2, 3, 4, 5", and place a small Δn in plex 13 at the three corrected features (Profile Shoal, the anchorage, ideal-url-organizer I.); (b) end each of notices 1–5 with "Δ on sheet 1" linked to `#top`; (c) collapse 6–9 into one line, "rustmapper 0.1.0–0.1.3 on PyPI · Rust-sitemap, 8 Nov 2025", set as a `<sub>` below the list so "Corrected through Notice 9" stays honest while the heading's five stays five.
Cost: S for (a) and (c); M for (b) because the renderer has to know which notice corrected which feature.

**3. The chart is finished before the vessel arrives; the hero's motion is a ship sailing into a done chart.**
Where: frames/hero/strip.png. t=0: neat line, name, thesis, every sounding, every island outline, both land masses, title block, source diagram, all present; only the blue tints are missing. t=4.1 s: tints in. t=24 s: boat mid-course. t=28.1 s: boat anchored at Scrapy Harbor. t=48 s onward: identical to the still.
Why it matters: STD 8, J8: "the chart surveys itself: contours by depth, soundings in course order, name set, boat sails". The soundings are the survey; if they are all present at t=0, the 24-second sail has no job. The t=0 frame also fails "every frame is a finished sheet": a chart with soundings and no tints is an uncoloured proof.
Fix: stagger the soundings' opacity along the course. Keep the name, thesis and title block static from t=0; begin contours by depth at 0–4 s as now; then give each sounding and each island name `begin = 4s + k×0.5s` in order of distance along the boat's track (nearest the entry point first), so the figures appear as the boat passes them, finishing with Scrapy Harbor's 265₅ as it anchors. The approaches sheet already does this in principle; the hero should do it visibly.
Cost: M.

**4. Languages and stack are said five times in one screen, and two subs explain the trick.**
Where: readme-light-0 title block "CRAWL AND DATA INFRASTRUCTURE · PYTHON AND RUST" (hero x≈70–300,y≈395); table rows Languages and Stack (y≈810–880); readme-light-4: Instruments intro "Python and Rust most days; Swift, C, TypeScript and Go…" (y≈490), the Instruments sheet (y≈520–730), the mirror `<sub>` (y≈755–785). Self-explaining subs: under the hero (y≈650) "The chart number is the public repository count; the edition is the rustmapper release"; under the approaches (readme-light-2, y≈250) "The legend on this sheet defines every symbol used on the page and says which figures are measured".
Why it matters: STD 9 (copy says each thing once; no self-explanation of the tricks), J9. Repetition is how a page tells the reader it does not trust him to have read; it costs the second-look layers their surprise (the chart number = repo count is a found thing until the caption hands it over).
Fix: delete the Instruments intro sentence; keep the mirror `<sub>` as the one textual duplicate (it earns its place as searchable text) and let the table carry the recruiter's copy. Delete the hero sub's second sentence and move "the chart number includes this repository" to the colophon's Figures bullet, where T8 already had it. Shorten the approaches sub to "Soundings in URLs."; the legend's own header already says EVERY SHEET.
Cost: S.

**5. The variation layer is captioned on the sheet and never resolved.**
Where: hero-day x≈635–725,y≈178–190: "VAR 14h (2026) / AUTHOR'S LOCAL TIME" (hero-rose-band crop). hero-phone already has it right: "VAR 14h (2026)" alone. The colophon's Variation bullet, which should answer the question, is missing (defect 1).
Why it matters: STD 13 ("the compass variation note that only resolves in the colophon", none captioned), J12. The sheet answers its own riddle in the next line, so the colophon has nothing left to resolve, and at the moment the colophon does not exist anyway. This was the clearest planned collectible on the page and it is spent on arrival.
Fix: remove "AUTHOR'S LOCAL TIME" from the desktop hero (two editions × two motion variants); restore the Variation bullet from T8 line 165 ("…turned so the busiest hour sits at north. That is the variation.") and let it also carry the night edition's reason in the same sentence, as my panel document asked. One line; said once; at the end.
Cost: S.

## 4. Three things that must not be touched

1. **The footer sheet as drawn** (sheets/footer-day.png, crops/footer.png). "The chart ends here. The web doesn't." set in the serif italic; the sea drawn as a single line that turns and falls at the LIMIT OF SURVEY 2026; the same hatch and the same rotated text as the hero's margin, so on a scroll-up the margin becomes the fall (my F2, built); the boat anchored short of the edge over "14 · good holding" in italics; the Index of Sheets with 6 shaded; the serpent at 84 s with a 20-second ripple and "Obstn rep. 2026 (PA)" as its trace (my F3, built). Only its placement on the page is wrong. Move it; do not redraw it.
2. **The log's last two beats** (crops/log-left.png, log-phone.png). "1431 · Nothing on fire." after "1430 · remarks · nothing to report" is the two-beat joke in the form, and the closing line "1947 UTC · watch kept by cron · 1,665 commits · 21 repositories▮" with the blinking cursor is the one place the page ends at a position rather than a sign-off. It is live data in a diegetic voice and it points forward. The phone edition keeps exactly these lines and nothing else, which is the right cut.
3. **The cross-sheet hand-offs**: the dashed "SEE SHEET 3" inset around Scrapy Harbor on the hero (hero-entrance crop) that the Approaches sheet then opens; the Wk '25 "fix/144-lean-docker-extras" wreck with a dead branch as its date; "Local knowledge advised · see Notices 1–5" on the approaches. These make six sheets one chart, which is what the adjoining-sheet idea in my panel document wanted. Close the loop upward (defect 2) but do not remove the downward references.

## 5. Does it look like a team spent months on it?

The sheets do. The approaches chart is the proof: a legend of 36 symbols each with one phrase, a wreck that is a dead branch, a channel "charted as proposed; nothing runs it yet", lights that are the only saturated colour at night, and a phone edition redrawn rather than shrunk. The hero's title block could be photographed on a real chart. The log is a document the character wrote. Where it still looks like a template is in the Markdown between the sheets and at the ends: the three-row table under the hero and the two bulleted feature lists are a profile README's skeleton showing through the chart, the Instruments sheet is three rotated words beside three dotted-leader lists, which is a styled table and reads as a diagram, the Notices list turns into a changelog at item 6, and the page's last visible element is a collapsed disclosure triangle labelled Colophon. The thing that would make it read as months of work is pacing: a page that rewards a second scroll-up is made by someone who expects to be read twice. Right now the second reading has nowhere to go, because the ending that would send a reader back up to the margin is the one sheet not on the page. Restore it, close the loop between the margin tally and the Notices, and let the vessel actually sound the hero, and the three audiences I named in panel30 (ten seconds, two minutes, source view) each get their layer.
