## 2. The five-second test

**870 px, day.** A cream Admiralty-style sheet with a very large serif name and one italic sentence. The eye goes name → sentence → the biggest island, which is labelled GAME ENGINE I., then SCRAPY HARBOR with the boat and the two buoys. In five seconds I know the name, that he "surveys" something and that the web is "wrong about itself"; I half-believe he is either a Scrapy person or a game-engine person, because those are the two largest labels in the picture and the field line (CRAWL AND DATA INFRASTRUCTURE · PYTHON AND RUST) is 8 px stick lettering in the title block. The field is actually acquired from the HTML table two lines below the image, not from the sheet.

**360 px.** The phone edition is better than the desktop at the five-second job: name and thesis fill the top third, the title block is centred and large (THE OPEN WEB / CRAWL AND DATA INFRASTRUCTURE · PYTHON AND RUST / SOUNDINGS IN COMMITS · DATUM: MAIN / CHART NO. 21 · EDITION 0.1.3 · NOTICE 9), the lights and shoals survive, one light flares. Two losses: the word "rustmapper" is gone from the phone hero entirely (the survey area has figures but no name), and "SMALL-SCALE" has replaced LIMIT OF SURVEY as the rotated margin note, which is correct chart-speak but unglossed and reads as an apology.

**Night.** The sheet reads as a chart lit from the buoys outward: magenta flares, white name, navy water. The person reads as careful and slightly severe. The thesis sentence is a little low in contrast on the hero at night (grey-blue italic on navy) but still read inside five seconds.

## 3. Top five defects, ranked

**1. The sheet contradicts itself about its own corrections.** Where: hero bottom margin, "CORRECTED THROUGH NOTICE 9" in the title block (x 120–300, y 440) against "SMALL CORRECTIONS 2026 — 169, 170, 171, 172, 173" outside the neat line bottom-right (x 640–860, y 495); footer repeats "9 notices · corrected through Notice 9". On a real sheet the small-corrections line lists the Notice numbers applied, so a sheet corrected through Notice 9 cannot carry corrections 169–173. A hydrographer (J4) reads this in two seconds as the one line that was not understood, and it undoes the credibility the rest of the furniture buys. The placement is also swapped: Admiralty practice puts small corrections bottom-left and the chart number bottom-right (the number is already correct top-right). Fix: either list the real Notice numbers ("Small corrections 2026 — 5, 6, 7, 8, 9") or, if 169–173 are the README's commit or PR numbers and that is the joke, label them so ("Small corrections 2026 — commits 169–173") and raise the title block to "Corrected through Notice 173"; move the line to bottom-left and "CHART NO. 21 · SHEET 1" to bottom-right. Cost: S.

**2. A third numeral class on the hero that the legend does not define, colliding with itself.** Where: rustmapper's survey area, hero x 560–720, y 170–360 (hero-entrance crop x 420–720, y 0–200): grey, upright, unsubscripted figures 358, 146, 108, 73, 37, 17, 15, 13, 1, 0, 0, 0 and 296, inside doubled dashed outlines that are two offset copies of the same shape. check.md confirms the collisions (BOUNDS-TEXTURE, five pairs 11–19 px apart on every hero edition). Why it matters: STD 1/4 and J4 — the page's whole honesty is one lettering rule (upright measured, sloping not), and here is a grey upright figure that is neither in the legend's "Height · commits, months" nor "Sounding · 1000s, 100s"; the doubled outlines read as misregistration, the one thing a printed chart never shows. Fix: keep the six non-zero weekly figures only, set them in the sounding style (slanted, no subscript) and add one legend line "grey figures · commits by week, 2026 survey lines"; collapse each doubled outline to a single dashed "Approximate contour" (already in the legend). Cost: M.

**3. Coastal hachure rings on every island.** Where: hero, the tick ring on GAME ENGINE I. (x 520–600, y 190–270), SCRAPY HARBOR (x 465–545, y 350–430) and all seventeen islets down to 7₄ and 4₂ (hero-entrance crop shows the ring on 22₃ at x 0–30). Why it matters: the ticked shoreline is copperplate "sanding" from engraved sheets, 1800–1900; the page otherwise commits to the late paper chart (IALA B, Fl characters, magenta lights, blue tints under 5 and 10, stick-lettered notes). It is the one prop left from the wrong century, and at phone size it turns every island into an eye or a gear, so the picture reads as "ringed targets" instead of "land in water" (J2 at 360). Fix: plain coastline plus one blue tint band for every island; if a ring is wanted on the two large islands keep it as a second, lighter contour line without ticks. Cost: S.

**4. The approaches legend and title block do not survive their media.** Where: approaches-day legend tray y 480–640, type roughly 7–8 px at 870; approaches-phone title "Approaches to Scrapy Harbor · Surveyed by rustmapper · Datum: main" inside a ruled box over a hatched header (y 50–190). Why it matters: STD 5 (semantic text ≥ 13 px at 1280, ≈ 9 px in the README column) and J5; and the boxed, framed title on the phone is exactly the "attribution panel" object the hero correctly abandoned — the one sheet that still has a frame around its title is the climax sheet. Fix: on the desktop sheet merge the three light entries (Light / Flare / Halo) into one line and the four doubtfuls (Rep / ED / SD / PA) into two, drop the sloop and packet-boat lines (they are self-evident), and raise the remaining 28 lines to 9 px; on the phone remove the box and the hatch behind the title, centre-stack it open like the hero, and move LIMIT OF SURVEY 2026 up to where the hatch was. Cost: M.

**5. Upright bearings that nobody measured.** Where: hero 233° (x 640 y 253, no line attached), 208° and 290° (hero-entrance crop x 340–320, y 160 and y 350); approaches 324° (x 400 y 423) and "Health Ldg Lts 290°" (x 265 y 338). Why it matters: J7 and STD 1 — the page says upright means measured; a bearing between two repositories is a drawn angle. The leading line 290° has a line and a legend entry and is defensible as a drawn construction; the others are decoration wearing the measured face. Fix: set 233° to a sloping figure or delete it (it belongs to nothing); keep 290° on both sheets; set 208° and 324° sloping. Cost: S.

Also noted, below the top five: the phone edition of sheet 5 (instruments-phone.png) is a 72-px strip containing only a rule and "CHART NO. 21 · SHEET 5" — a sheet with no content, even if the HTML carries the list below it; the unit caution "SOUNDINGS IN COMMITS" outside the neat line is set in grey where the era set it in magenta (the only coloured margin text of the metric changeover); and rustmapper's name is absent from the hero phone edition.

## 4. Three things that must not be touched

1. **The hero's marginal furniture.** Unit caution top centre outside the neat line; chart number top-right outside it; imprint bottom centre outside it ("PUBLISHED AT GITHUB.COM/BENJAMINSRUSSELL · 7 OCT 2026 · SUPERINTENDENCE: BUILD_ASSETS.PY"); open, centre-stacked, unframed title block with region, field, unit, datum, number, edition, IALA region; source diagram A/B/C beside it. Every slot is the slot a hydrographic office used, and every line in it is literally true of the work. This is the page's claim to being a chart rather than a chart-themed graphic, and it is now correct.
2. **The legend's one honesty rule and its completeness.** "upright figures are measured · sloping figures are not · underlined: above datum", then every symbol on the page once, with the S-4 meaning and the meaning here (Wk · dead branch, year; Rep · reported, not found; ED · existence doubtful; Hatch · unsurveyed; Danger line · shoal). The abbreviations are the real ones. Fix the type size (defect 4) but do not add, rename or re-explain a single entry.
3. **Night as a redrawn sheet with magenta lights.** Slate land, navy water, lighter tint on the shallows, red north arrow kept, magenta flares and halos as the only saturated colour. It is designed, it is historically the right colour for lights, and it makes the lights the brightest things on the page without saying so. Keep the palette and the halo radius exactly as they are.

## 5. Does it look like a team spent months on it?

Yes, mostly, and for the first time it looks like one team working in one decade: the imprint, the small-corrections slot, the unit caution, IALA B, light characters, zones of confidence, the wreck and the doubtful-position abbreviations all belong to the last paper charts, and the display serif of the name is the single allowed anachronism standing in for engraved title lettering. The six sheets have six silhouettes and the footer's line going over the edge with a boat anchored in 14 of good holding is a genuinely good ending. What still reads as template or diagram: the soundings sheet's 3×7 grid of repository cells with a sparkline in each is a Grafana panel that has been printed on cream; the instruments sheet's three columns of dotted leaders with little icons is the "skills" block of a résumé, and its phone edition is empty; and the Scrapy Harbor shore on sheet 3, with stage1_discovery, stage2_page_analysis, stage4_summaries, Redis · queues and PostgreSQL · metrics as labelled rectangles joined by a straight dark line, is a system-architecture diagram that has been given a coastline rather than a harbour that happens to contain a system. The contradiction in the corrections line (defect 1) and the undefined grey figures (defect 2) are the two places where the costume slips and shows that the conventions were reproduced rather than understood; fix those and the sheet stops pretending to be a chart and simply is one.
